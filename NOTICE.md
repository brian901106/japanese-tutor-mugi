## soundtouchjs (LGPL-2.1)

- Version 0.3.0. Used by `src/game/voiceFx.ts` to change the speed and pitch of spoken replies on the phone.
- Packaged as a separate file: `vite.config.ts` puts it in its own chunk, so the production build writes it to
  `dist/assets/soundtouch-*.js`. It is not merged into the application code, so you can replace that file with another
  build of the library (or a relinked version) without touching the rest of the app.
- Source code: <https://github.com/cutterbl/SoundTouchJS> (the `repository` field of `node_modules/soundtouchjs/package.json`).
- License text: [licenses/soundtouchjs-LGPL-2.1.txt](licenses/soundtouchjs-LGPL-2.1.txt).

## External services (not distributed with this project)

Each service has its own terms of use. Each one you turn on needs your own account or API key.

- **Anthropic API**: the chat, the 速查 lookup and the 小提醒 rewrite. Its terms apply to what you send and receive.
- **OpenAI** (ChatGPT sign-in, optional): the ChatGPT chat option, with your own ChatGPT account. Its terms apply to what you send and receive.
- **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech** and **Azure AI Speech**: optional cloud voices.
- **VOICEVOX** (the engine and its voice characters): optional local voices. The engine is not included. Each engine and each
  character has its own terms of use, and you must follow them. Credit the character by name in the form
  `VOICEVOX:<character name>`, as its terms require.
